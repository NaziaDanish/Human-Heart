Purkinje Fibers

Purkinje fibers are a special network of fibers found in the inner ventricular walls of the heart. They are part of the heart's electrical conduction system and are responsible for carrying the electrical signals from the Bundle of His deep into the muscular walls (myocardium) of the ventricles.

Function of Purkinje Fibers:

  • They rapidly conduct the electrical impulse throughout the ventricles.
  • This ensures that both the left and right ventricles contract at the same time.
  • The synchronized contraction helps pump blood efficiently to the lungs and the rest of the body.

Location:

Purkinje fibers are located at the base and inner walls of the ventricles and are the final link in the electrical conduction pathway of the heart.

Interesting Fact:

These fibers are named after the Czech scientist **Jan Evangelista Purkyně**, who discovered them in 1839.
